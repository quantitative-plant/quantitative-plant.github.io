This algorithm analyses images produced by a flat-bed scanner with seeds layed over the glass surface. Analysis outlines detected seeds and computes their geometrical properties.

Works as a Bisque module into the iPlant infrastructure.

## Notes

Works in a web browser. Requires an internet connection. Works as a Bisque module.
