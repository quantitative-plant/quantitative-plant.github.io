VitiCanopy is a tool that growers, irrigation practitioners and scientists can use to quickly and reliably assess spatial and temporal growth and canopy architecture dynamics. This can then be associated with final yield and grape quality. The app was released in September 2015, and was funded by the Australian Grape and Wine Authority. An android version of the app is under development.
