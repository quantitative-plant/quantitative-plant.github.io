RootLM is a simple color image analysis program, in combination of a modified marking technique of root growth on the surface of a Petri dish, for root length measurement. RootLM provides an alternative tool to quickly generate accurate and reproducible root growth measurement with a minimum equipment requirement. 

RootLM is free for public download.
