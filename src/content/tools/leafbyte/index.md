LeafByte measures leaf area and herbivory quickly and accurately. Take a picture of a leaf and a scale to measure total leaf area and leaf area consumed.

<ul>
<li>Saves your data in a convenient spreadsheet on your phone or in Google Drive</li>
<li>Analyzes leaves of almost any size or shape</li>
<li>Uses freehand drawing to reconstruct missing leaf margins</li>
<li>Can measure dark plant material (such as leaves) on a white background or light plant material (such as petals) on a dark background</li>
<li>Reads and records all major barcodes</li>
<li>Automatically corrects skew in photos taken at an angle</li>
</ul>
