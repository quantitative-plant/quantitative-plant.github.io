The Leaf Angle Distribution Toolbox is a software package, which provides tools for the quantification of leaf surface
properties within natural canopies via 3-d reconstruction from stereo images. The approach includes
a semi-automatic selection process of single leaves and different modes of surface characterization
via polygon smoothing or surface model fitting. Based on the resulting surface meshes leaf angle
statistics are computed on the whole-leaf level or from local derivations.
