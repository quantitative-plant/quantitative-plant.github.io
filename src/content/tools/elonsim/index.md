ElonSim is a simulator of plant growth coupled to a simulator of the image acquisition to generate images of simulated plants associated with a known synthetic ground truth. The simulator incorporates parameters of the plant and parameters of the experimental imaging system acquiring the images. This opens the possibility to assess the impact of these parameters on the performance of any segmentation algorithm on unlimited populations of virtual plants.

## Notes

Not an image analysis tool, but and image simulation tool
