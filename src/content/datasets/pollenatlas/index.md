Pollen Atlas: Light microscopic images of pollen and spores
In the collection mainly seed plants (Spermatophyta) are found. There are also some fern plants (Pteridophyta) and few mosses (Bryophyta) and fungi (Mycophyta). 
Poll classification according to Beug HJ "Guideline for the determination of pollen for Central Europe and adjacent areas".
